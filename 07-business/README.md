## Business Model 1: AI Freelancer

### Getting Started (Week 1-2)

#### Step 1: Define Your Offer

Pick ONE service to start with. Ask Claude:

```
I want to start freelancing as an AI consultant.
Based on these skills I've developed:
- Prompt engineering and custom instructions
- Building websites with Claude
- Setting up n8n automations
- Creating AI agents

Help me define a clear, specific service offering
that I can start selling this week. Include:
- Service name
- One-paragraph description
- What the client gets (deliverables)
- Timeline
- Price (3 tiers)
- Ideal client profile
```

#### Step 2: Create Your Portfolio

You don't need past clients. Build portfolio pieces:

```
Use Claude to build:
1. A case study showing a hypothetical client scenario
2. Before/after examples of your work
3. A professional one-page service description
4. A personal website showcasing your services
```

#### Step 3: Find Your First Clients

**Best channels for AI services:**

| Channel | Approach | Expected Results |
|---------|----------|-----------------|
| **LinkedIn** | Post AI tips daily, DM business owners | 1-3 leads/week |
| **Local businesses** | Walk in or email with a specific proposal | 1-2 meetings/week |
| **Freelance platforms** | Upwork, Fiverr, Malt (France) | Competitive but volume |
| **Communities** | Skool, Facebook groups, Slack communities | Warm leads through value |
| **Referrals** | Ask satisfied clients to refer you | Highest conversion rate |

#### Step 4: Your Outreach Script

Use Claude to generate personalized outreach:

```
Write a cold outreach message for a [type of business]
owner. I want to offer my AI automation service.

My approach:
1. Mention something specific about their business
   (I'll fill in the blank)
2. Identify a pain point AI can solve
3. Offer a free 15-minute audit
4. Keep it under 100 words
5. No salesy language — be helpful and genuine

Business type: [e.g., real estate agency, e-commerce store,
marketing agency]
```

Instead of custom work every time, create repeatable packages:

### Example: "AI Business Starter Pack"

```

PRICE: $997 (one-time)
DELIVERY: 5 business days

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
1. AI Audit (2-hour discovery call + written report)
   - Map current workflows
   - Identify top 5 automation opportunities
   - Calculate potential time/money savings

2. Custom Claude Project Setup
   - Configured project with custom instructions
   - Knowledge base loaded with business documents
   - 5 ready-to-use prompt templates

3. One Automation Workflow
   - Built and tested in n8n
   - Connected to client's existing tools
   - Documentation + training video

4. Training Session (1 hour)
   - How to use their new AI tools

   - Q&A
```

## Pricing Strategy

### The Value-Based Pricing Framework

Don't charge for your time. Charge for the value you create.

```
CLIENT SITUATION:
- Employee spends 15 hours/week on manual data entry
- Employee salary: $25/hour
- Annual cost of this task: 15 × $25 × 52 = $19,500/year

YOUR AUTOMATION:
- Reduces task to 2 hours/week
- Saves 13 hours/week = $16,900/year

YOUR PRICE:
- Charge 10-20% of annual savings
- Price: $1,690 – $3,380 (one-time)
- Client saves $13,500+ in year 1 alone

VALUE RATIO: Client pays $3,000, saves $16,900 = 5.6x ROI
```

### Pricing Tiers Template

| Tier | Name | Price | What's Included |
|------|------|-------|----------------|
| **Basic** | Starter | $X | Core deliverable only |
| **Standard** | Professional | $2X | Core + extras + support |
| **Premium** | Enterprise | $4X | Everything + priority + training |

Always offer 3 tiers. Most clients choose the middle one.
